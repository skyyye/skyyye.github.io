# About Me

I am a third-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the School of Computing and Information Systems, Singapore Management University, under the supervision of [Prof. Tony Tang](https://hcitang.github.io/) and [Prof. Jiannan Li](https://jchrisli.github.io/). I received my master’s degree in Computer Science and bachelor’s degree in Information Security from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Before pursuing my PhD, I worked as a software engineer at UNISOC.

My research focuses on the design of context-aware AI systems that can understand and adapt to different forms of context, including physical contexts (e.g., virtual and real-world environments), social contexts (e.g., group interactions and intimate relationships), and professional contexts (e.g., interdisciplinary collaboration, education, and design processes).
